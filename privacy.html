PRIVACY

Your camera stays yours.

Eye Flight is a client-side gaze-controlled flight game. It does not contain an account system, camera-upload backend, or frame-storage service.

Camera access

The game asks for camera permission only after you choose camera play. Camera frames are processed in your browser to estimate face and eye landmarks. Eye Flight does not intentionally save or upload those frames.

Gaze engine

Eye Flight loads MediaPipe Tasks Vision runtime files from jsDelivr and a face-landmarker model from Google-hosted model storage. MediaPipe documentation states that image/video input is processed on-device rather than sent to Google servers.

MediaPipe also states that Tasks APIs may send API performance and utilization metrics to Google. Operators of a public deployment should review the current third-party terms and any consent obligations that apply to their users.

Permissions

Your browser and operating system control camera access. You can revoke permission at any time through browser site settings or operating-system privacy settings.

Hosting

If this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub serves the static site and may apply its own platform logging and policies separately from the Eye Flight application code.

Eye Flight is an entertainment experience, not a medical device or laboratory-grade eye tracker.
